I am an EEE-Physics double major student working on subjects located at the middle ground of ML and information theory. 

I did work on the mathematical foundations of ML, advised by Orhan Arıkan and with Tolga Mete Duman I worked on finding new bounds for the capacity of the binary deletion channel.

I also work on knowledge graph completion and goal planning for robotics in my free time.

See [glen](https://www.neuromorphic.studio)
